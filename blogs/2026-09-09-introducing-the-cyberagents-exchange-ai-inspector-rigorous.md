Open-source registries for AI agents are only effective when they include a rigorous, transparent security review process for community submissions. That’s why for its new CyberAgents Exchange registry, Tenable paired its exposure management expertise with OpenAI GPT Cyber models to create the CyberAgents Exchange AI Inspector. Key takeaways The Exchange Inspector combines Tenable’s exposure detection with OpenAI’s GPT Cyber models and with human oversight to rigorously vet submissions made to the CyberAgents Exchange.
