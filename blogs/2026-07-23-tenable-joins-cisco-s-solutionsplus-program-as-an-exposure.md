Tenable® Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: TENB), the exposure management company , today announced that it has joined Cisco’s SolutionsPlus program to offer its unified exposure management capabilities to Cisco customers. This partnership provides global enterprises with a fast, proven transition to an industry-leading exposure management platform without losing critical visibility into enterprise risk.
