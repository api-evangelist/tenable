Tenable® Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: TENB), the exposure management company , today launched the CyberAgents Exchange , a new open-source AI exchange created to foster industry collaboration and improve collective cyber defense. The CyberAgents Exchange, powered by Tenable, is the only purpose-built, cybersecurity-native registry for AI agents, skills, MCP servers and multi-agent playbooks in the current market.
