Tenable® Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: TENB), the exposure management company , today announced the expansion of the CyberAgents Exchange , powered by Tenable, to further accelerate enterprise security adoption of open source AI agents, skills and MCP servers. Engineered by Tenable using OpenAI GPT cyber models, the CyberAgents Exchange AI Inspector (Exchange Inspector) evaluates selected AI components listed on the Exchange, including an enterprise-grade safety review, which is designed to help organizations more securely deploy in production environments.
