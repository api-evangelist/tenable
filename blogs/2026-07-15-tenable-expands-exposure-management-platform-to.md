Tenable® Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: TENB), the exposure management company , today announced the expansion of the Tenable One Exposure Management Platform, unifying application security risks with all other exposure data. By integrating static code vulnerability data, Tenable One delivers complete, code-to-runtime visibility across the entire attack surface.
