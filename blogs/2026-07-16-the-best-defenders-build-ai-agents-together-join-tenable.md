Agentic AI use is exploding, yet most security teams are building agents in isolation. Tenable is hosting SWARM, a build event at Black Hat 2026, for security practitioners to create and collaborate on agentic, open-source tooling to drive collective defense and stop adversaries together. Key takeaways According to Gartner®, by 2028, an average global Fortune 500 enterprise will have more than 150,000 AI agents in use, up from less than 15 in 2025, generating significant agent sprawl, IT complexity, and management challenges.
